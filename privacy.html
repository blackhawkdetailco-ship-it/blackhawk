Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Blackhawk Detail Co is a mobile auto detailing business serving the Tri-Valley area of California. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to give you a quote and show up at the right place:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number and email address
  • Your city or service address
  • Your vehicle year, make, and model
  • The services you are interested in

We do not collect payment card numbers through this website or through our ad forms.

How we collect it

You give it to us directly, through one of these:

  • The contact or quote form on this website
  • A lead form attached to one of our ads on Facebook or Instagram
  • Direct messages, texts, or calls

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, give you a quote, schedule your appointment, and follow up about your service. That is the whole list.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not rent it, trade it, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our website host and our scheduling or messaging tools, and only to the extent they need it to do that job. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Text messages and calls

When you submit your phone number, you are agreeing to let us contact you by call or text about your quote and your appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts from us.

Ads on Facebook and Instagram

We advertise on Meta platforms. If you fill out a form inside one of our ads, Meta passes that information to us and also handles it under its own privacy policy. You can review it at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

How long we keep it

We keep customer records so we can service your vehicle again and honor any coating or correction work we have done. If you would rather we did not, tell us and we will delete your information.

Your choices

You can ask us to send you a copy of what we have, correct anything that is wrong, or delete your information entirely. Email or text us and we will take care of it.

Children

This website and our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
